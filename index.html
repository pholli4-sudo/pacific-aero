Who we represent

A few suppliers, never two that compete

American OEMs buy from suppliers they trust, and trust is built in person over years. We put that presence to work for a handful of Chinese manufacturers at a time, and we never represent two suppliers to the same customer for the same commodity.

Certified quality systemISO 9001 as a minimum. AS9100D for aerospace work, NADCAP for special processes, API or ASME where the buyer's category calls for it.
Export experienceYou have shipped to overseas customers before and can document it. A first export should not be an American OEM.
An English-capable quality teamSupplier approval is a documentation exercise. Your quality engineers must be able to answer an American auditor directly.
Capacity and intentRoom to take on new work, and the patience to invest twelve to eighteen months before the first award.
Commodities we knowPrecision machining, castings and forgings, fabrications and weldments, composites, fasteners and hardware, harnesses and electrical assemblies, tooling.

What we do

From first introduction to the tenth reorder

Winning an American award is a sequence of small, specific tasks spread over a year or more. We do them with you, in the order the buyer expects, and we are the face the buyer sees between your visits.

Readiness review

A frank assessment of where you stand against the buyer's supplier requirements before anyone is introduced: certifications, capacity, documentation, pricing position and the gaps to close first.

Written report · 2 weeks

Account targeting and introductions

Which American buyers actually source your commodity, who decides, and what they are short of. Introductions made in person to sourcing, supplier quality and engineering, with your capability presented in their language.

Target list · in-person meetings

Supplier approval

Supplier surveys, self-assessments, document packages and on-site audit preparation. We rehearse the audit with your team and attend it, so nothing is lost in translation.

AS9100D · ISO 9001 · customer supplier manuals

RFQ and quoting

RFQ packages explained, drawings and specifications clarified with the buyer, design-for-manufacture feedback returned the way American engineers expect, and quotes positioned to win without leaving money behind.

Landed cost · HTS and Section 301 tariffs · Incoterms 2020

First article and award

AS9102 or PPAP first-article planning, witnessed inspections, and negotiation of long-term agreement terms, payment terms and tooling ownership before you sign.

AS9102 Rev C · PPAP · LTA terms

Account stewardship

After the award we stay on the account: delivery tracking, quality escapes handled fast and honestly, quarterly reviews with the buyer, and the next RFQ before it goes to your competitors.

Monthly scorecard · quarterly business review
How an award happens

Twelve to eighteen months, honestly stated

No American OEM awards production work to a new overseas supplier in a quarter. This is the realistic path for a machined-part or casting supplier with its certifications already in place.

Step 1

Readiness review

Certifications, capacity and documentation assessed against the target buyers' supplier requirements. Gaps closed before any introduction.

Weeks 1 to 2
Step 2

Targets and introductions

Three to five buyers selected. In-person introductions to sourcing and supplier quality. Capability presentation and plant video reviewed by their engineers.

Months 1 to 3
Step 3

Supplier approval

Supplier survey, document package and on-site audit. We prepare your team, attend the audit and manage the corrective actions.

Months 3 to 6
Step 4

RFQ, first article, award

Quotes on the buyer's first packages, first-article inspection witnessed, long-term agreement negotiated and signed.

Months 6 to 12
Step 5

Stewardship

Deliveries tracked, issues handled in the buyer's time zone, quarterly reviews held, and the next package pursued.
